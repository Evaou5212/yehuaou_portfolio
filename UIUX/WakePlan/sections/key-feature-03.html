Key Feature 03

Personalized Future-Self Reminders

Rather than relying on generic alarm sounds, users create reminders specifically for themselves.

They can either:

  • Record their own voice, or
  • Write a text message that will be converted into speech.

These reminders reinforce personal intention instead of simply adding more noise.

Examples include:

  • "Wake up! You have a meeting at 9."
  • "Don't miss your flight."
  • "It's leg day—get to the gym!"
