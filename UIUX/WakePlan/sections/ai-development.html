AI-Assisted Development

WakePlan was independently designed and built using AI-assisted development workflows.

AI accelerated implementation by helping prototype interfaces, generate code, iterate on interaction logic, and rapidly test ideas. Rather than replacing design decisions, AI served as a collaborative tool that enabled faster experimentation while keeping human-centered product thinking at the core.

This project demonstrates how modern AI tools can empower individual designers to take an idea from concept to working product in a significantly shorter development cycle.
