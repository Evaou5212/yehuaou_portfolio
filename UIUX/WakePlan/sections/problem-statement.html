Problem Statement

Existing alarm apps fail to address unconscious behavior.

Through personal experience and observation, I realized that adding more alarms does not necessarily improve wake-up success. Instead, users often:

  • Repeatedly dismiss alarms without conscious awareness.
  • Lose track of how many alarms remain.
  • Accidentally dismiss the final alarm.
  • Wake up too late and miss important events.

The fundamental problem is behavioral rather than technical.

People don't need more alarms. They need more awareness.
