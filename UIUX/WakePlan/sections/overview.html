Most alarm apps assume users are fully conscious when they hear an alarm. In reality, many people operate on autopilot while half asleep, dismissing alarms without thinking and often oversleeping as a result. WakePlan reimagines waking up as a structured process rather than a series of repetitive notifications.

Built around the concept of a Wake Plan—a progressive sequence of time-aware alerts paired with personalized reminders—WakePlan restores awareness during the wake-up window instead of relying on more alarms. The project is a fully functional web application, designed and developed end-to-end with AI-assisted prototyping.
