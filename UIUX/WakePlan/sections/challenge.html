Challenge

Many people rely on setting multiple alarms to wake up on time. However, when they are still sleepy, they often dismiss alarms automatically through muscle memory without consciously processing them. In many cases, they unknowingly turn off the final alarm and oversleep, missing meetings, classes, flights, or other important commitments.

The issue is not the number of alarms—it is the temporary loss of awareness during the wake-up process.

The Solution

WakePlan introduces the concept of a Wake Plan: a structured sequence of progressively timed alarms centered around a target wake-up time. Instead of repeatedly playing the same ringtone, each alarm announces the current time, reminds users how much time remains until their target wake-up time, and then delivers a personalized reminder created by the user.

At the target wake-up time, the interaction escalates by requiring a simple wake-up task before the alarm can be dismissed, preventing unconscious oversleeping.
