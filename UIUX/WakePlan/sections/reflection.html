Reflection

WakePlan began as a solution to my own daily frustration but evolved into an exploration of behavioral design.

The biggest lesson from this project is that solving user problems often requires reframing them. Oversleeping is not simply caused by weak alarms—it is caused by temporary loss of awareness. By designing interactions that restore users' understanding of both time and purpose, technology can better support human decision-making instead of simply demanding attention.

Ultimately, WakePlan is less about alarms and more about helping people regain control at the exact moment they are most likely to lose it.
