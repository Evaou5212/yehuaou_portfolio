Key Feature 02

Time-Aware Alarm Announcements

This is the core innovation of WakePlan.

Instead of immediately playing a ringtone or reminder, every alarm first announces:

"The current time is 7:30 AM. There are 30 minutes remaining until your target wake-up time."

Only after this announcement does the app play the user's personalized reminder.

For example:

"Wake up! You have an important meeting this morning."

This design continuously restores the user's awareness of both where they are in time and why they need to wake up, making unconscious dismissal significantly less likely.
