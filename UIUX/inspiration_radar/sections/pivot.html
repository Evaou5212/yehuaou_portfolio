Initial Idea & Pivot

New + popular is not the same as inspiring.

The original concept was simple: scan GitHub for the newest and hottest projects, then display them in a discovery feed that refreshed every six hours.

Testing revealed the gap. The system frequently surfaced technically popular repositories with little relevance to designers or creative coders. AI also struggled when given vague instructions such as “find creative or aesthetically interesting projects.”
