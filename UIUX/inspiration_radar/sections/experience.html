Designing the Discovery Experience

An archive, not another social feed.

Instead of competing with Pinterest, Product Hunt, or social media, the interface was designed as a continuously growing creative archive with its own identity.

  • Numbered archive entries and a database / catalog structure
  • Monospace metadata, thin borders, and a neutral visual system
  • Generated archive covers and an information-dense editorial layout

Progressive disclosure

The card interaction is designed as a three-step judgment path rather than a single dense dump of information.

Front — Identify

What is this project?

Title, cover, type, and a short snapshot so users can scan quickly.

Back — Judge

Why is it interesting?

AI-assisted notes on inspiration value, not just a README summary.

Detail — Explore

What else can I learn?

Links, related finds, and a path to keep exploring similar ideas.

Vibe-based discovery

Projects can be understood beyond technical categories. Filters such as playful, experimental, weird, and useful let users browse by creative character rather than framework or language.
