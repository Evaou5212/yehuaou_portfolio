Project Type

Mobile App UI/UX Design

Product Scope

Brand-led Oral Care Commerce Ecosystem

Role

UI/UX Designer

Focus Areas

Information Architecture, Visual System, Commerce Flow, Content Features, Loyalty Design

This project builds a complete mobile experience around the Yunnan Baiyao oral care product line. Rather than acting as a simple storefront, the product connects brand news, oral-care education, health-check guidance, product discovery, cart flow, loyalty points, and account services in one operational platform.

The core goal is to shorten the journey from brand awareness to product understanding, then to fast checkout and repeat purchase. A clear blue visual language communicates professionalism and trust, while modular blocks keep dense information easy to scan.

Project Background

From standalone product pages to a brand-content-commerce loop

The oral-care portfolio includes toothpaste, toothbrushes, mouthwash, and related products. Users need more than quick checkout: they need support choosing the right item, learning daily care methods, joining campaigns, and using points to increase repurchase intent. Based on these needs, the project introduces a multi-entry homepage and layered feature architecture that balances brand expression with commerce efficiency.

Design Challenge

How might we keep navigation clear and conversion smooth in a high-density interface?

Design Strategy

Four strategies shaped the end-to-end experience

01 Layered Dual Homepage

Homepage A focuses on launches and hero products, while Homepage B focuses on services and member benefits, reducing crowding per screen.

02 Parallel Commerce and Content Flows

Brand articles, oral-care classes, and health-check modules are embedded into the main flow so content directly supports decisions.

03 Standardized Commerce Modules

Listing, search, detail, and cart screens share one card and pricing structure to reduce cognitive switching costs.

04 Loyalty-Driven Retention

A points store and coupon mechanics extend user lifetime value and create a browse-buy-return loop.

Information Architecture

Homepage and catalog architecture for faster orientation

The dual-homepage system supports different user intents: page one emphasizes launches and promotions, while page two emphasizes brand services and loyalty entry points. The catalog combines vertical first-level navigation with a product grid to speed up browse-to-compare behavior.

Shopping Flow

A continuous conversion flow from search to checkout

Starting from keyword search, users can enter a relevant product stream and move into detail pages for variant confirmation, offer evaluation, and purchase action. Price, tags, guarantees, and cart or buy-now controls remain stable at key touchpoints.

  • Search: Supports both history and trending keywords to reduce input effort.
  • Product Feed: A two-column stream improves side-by-side comparison for similar items.
  • Detail: Key claims, promo pricing, and service guarantees reduce hesitation before purchase.

Content Ecosystem

Brand content, oral-care education, and health guidance

These features do more than communicate promotions; they explain why users should buy. Brand news adds credibility, oral-care lessons provide practical guidance, and health-check content adds professional authority and clear action cues.

Retention Loop

Cart, account, and points store drive retention

The purchase-retention loop is built by three modules: the cart captures checkout intent, the account center consolidates orders and service entries, and the points store turns engagement into redeemable value through rewards and coupons.

Design System

A blue visual system built around trust, clarity, and care

The palette transitions from light sky blue to deep brand blue, creating visual hierarchy from ambient background surfaces to high-priority actions: #D9F0FE for large surfaces, #A2BFEB for supporting blocks, #639ADD for functional emphasis, and #1944A0 for primary brand and CTA elements.

Reflection

From page design to ecosystem design

  • The value of this project is not a single polished page, but a scalable framework for oral-care content and commerce.
  • Consistent card patterns and navigation logic help users build predictable behavior across pages.
  • Future iterations can add ranking logic, A/B test metrics, and finer loyalty segmentation.
